Engineered a high-performing predictive framework using Random Forest and XGBoost to analyze a large-scale dataset of 140,000+ health records.

Achieved an AUC of 0.92 and 84% accuracy, optimizing the model for reliable clinical risk assessment.

Leveraged unsupervised clustering to segment patient data, delivering personalized health recommendations based on specific risk profiles.

Conducted feature importance analysis to identify critical biomarkers, finding that age, BMI, and mental health metrics were the primary drivers of cardiovascular risk.
